🌿 Guided Coping Resources

Evidence-based nervous system regulation tools you can practice right now:

4-7-8 Deep Box Breathing 2 Mins

Inhale gently for 4s, hold with soft awareness for 7s, exhale slowly for 8s. Resets the autonomic nervous system.

5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding 3 Mins

5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. Pulls the brain out of anxiety spirals.
